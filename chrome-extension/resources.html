Resources & privacy

Detection describes what the browser exposes. It does not establish ownership or permission.

Before you save

Save media only when permitted by law, the licence, the rights holder, and applicable platform terms. Verify your authority before starting a download.

Protected media

Video Pro Finder identifies public encryption and protection indicators, then blocks download. It does not retrieve keys, request licences, decrypt content, bypass protected playback, or capture decrypted output.

Look for the provider's official download options, contact the rights holder or support, or save a local reference to your library.

Supported and unsupported sources

Direct browser-exposed MP4, WebM, M4V, MOV, MP3, M4A, OGG, WAV, accessible non-protected HLS and DASH manifests, and subtitle sidecars can be identified. Browser and server restrictions may still prevent access.

Torrent files and magnet links are unsupported. DRM-protected media, authentication bypass, CORS bypass, signed-URL circumvention, and access-control evasion are not supported.

Privacy model

Source URLs, history, settings, and diagnostics are stored locally in this browser profile. No analytics or remote metadata uploads are enabled. The extension does not collect credentials, cookies, or unrelated page content.

Browser limitations

Cross-origin policy, login requirements, expiring URLs, native HLS support, DASH packaging, large files, browser download behavior, and service-worker suspension are controlled by the browser or provider. Estimates are not guarantees. No conversion, stream merging, or universal downloading is promised.

Authority declaration

Declarations are local user records and are not verified. A declaration never overrides protection blocking. Non-DRM does not mean unrestricted use.
